# FuturePath — West Africa Career Explorer

A WebXR career exploration app covering **57 careers** across **all 15 ECOWAS countries**.
Formal professions, informal trades, and traditional crafts — all in one place.

## What it does

- Explore careers by sector in an interactive 3D ring
- Filter by any of the 15 ECOWAS member states
- See typical tasks, skills, pathway, and certification for each career
- Add careers to a personal plan and download it as a Markdown file
- Works on desktop, tablet, and mobile browsers
- Enters VR automatically if a headset is connected

## Sectors covered

Formal Profession · Healthcare · Digital & Tech · Trades & Technical · Energy ·
Agriculture · Food & Hospitality · Textiles & Crafts · Beauty · Creative & Media ·
Business & Trade · Mining & Tourism
